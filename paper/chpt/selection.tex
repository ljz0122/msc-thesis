\chapter{Selection}

\section{Astrometry data sources and Target Selection}

The selection of candidate Quasars based on the position measurement from Gaia mission \citep{gaiacollaborationGaiaMission2016} and photometric data from SDSS and WISE. 

\subsection{Gaia}

Thanks for high precision position measurement of objects from Gaia. We are possible to filter the targets without proper motion in $3\sigma$. Our original selection based on Gaia EDR3 data from \citet{gaiacollaborationGaiaEarlyData2021a}.

The first selection based on the following SQL program:

\begin{minted}[]{SQL}
SELECT source_id, ra, dec, l, b, pmra, pmra_error, pmdec, pmdec_error, parallax, parallax_error, phot_g_mean_mag, phot_g_mean_flux, phot_g_mean_flux_error, phot_bp_mean_mag, phot_rp_mean_mag, bp_rp, ref_epoch
FROM gaiaedr3.gaia_source AS gaia 
WHERE gaia.ra>=190 AND gaia.ra<=210
  AND gaia.dec>22 AND gaia.dec<36
  AND gaia.phot_g_mean_mag<=19.
  AND abs((gaia.parallax)/gaia.parallax_error)<=2  
  AND (power(gaia.pmra/gaia.pmra_error,2) + power(gaia.pmdec/gaia.pmdec_error,2))<=4
\end{minted}

This code will select all objects in area around north galactic pole with zero parallax in 2-sigma.
